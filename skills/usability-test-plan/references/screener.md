# Screeners

A screener finds people who match the behaviour you need without telling them what you want to hear.

## Rules

- Ask about behaviour and recency: "In the last three months, which of these have you done?" not "Are you comfortable with mobile banking?"
- Multiple choice with plausible distractors. If the one you want is the only realistic answer, people will pick it.
- Hide the product and the topic. Ask about several activities so the target one does not stand out.
- Put disqualifiers early so people are not screened out after ten questions.
- Ask for frequency with ranges ("Never, once or twice, monthly, weekly or more").
- Include one open question that needs a specific answer ("Briefly describe the last time you sent money to someone overseas"). Vague answers flag professional testers.
- Standard exclusions: works in UX, design, research or marketing; works for the company or a competitor; took part in a study in the last six months.
- Ask about devices, assistive technology and access needs neutrally, so you can include people and set up sessions properly.

## Example

1. Which of these have you done in the last three months? (Select all that apply)
   - Bought something online from an overseas store
   - Sent money to someone in another country **(must select)**
   - Booked a flight
   - Paid a bill through a banking app
   - None of these **(screen out)**
2. How did you send it? (Select all)
   - Bank transfer through my bank's app or website
   - A money transfer app or website
   - A money transfer shop or agent
   - Someone else sent it for me **(screen out)**
3. Do you, or does anyone in your household, work in any of these? (Select all)
   - Banking or financial services **(screen out)**
   - Market research, UX or design **(screen out)**
   - Healthcare
   - Education
   - None of these
4. Briefly describe the last time you sent money to another country: who to, roughly how much, and how. (Open)

## Mix and quotas

State the mix as quotas: "3 who have used our app, 3 who have not; at least 2 on Android; at least 1 who uses a screen reader or magnification." Recruit one or two extra for no-shows.
