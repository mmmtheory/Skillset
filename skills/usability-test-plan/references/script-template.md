# Moderator script

Adapt the wording, keep the structure. Times are for a 45 minute moderated remote session.

## 1. Welcome and consent (5 min)

> Thanks for joining. I'm [name]. We're working on [general area, not the feature] and want to see how it works for people like you. We're testing the design, not you, so there are no wrong answers. If something is confusing, that's exactly what we need to know.
>
> I'll ask you to do a few things and to think out loud as you go. I may not answer questions during tasks, because we want to see what you'd do on your own. I'll explain anything afterwards.
>
> With your permission, I'll record the screen and audio. Only the research team will see it, and we'll delete it after [period]. Is that OK? You can stop at any time.

Get explicit consent on record. If recording is declined, take notes only.

## 2. Warm-up (5 min)

Ask about past behaviour related to the tasks, to build rapport and context:

- Tell me about the last time you [did the thing the tasks are about].
- What did you use? How did it go?

Avoid questions about the product being tested.

## 3. Tasks (25 to 30 min)

For each task:

1. Read the scenario aloud and, in remote sessions, paste it in the chat.
2. Ask them to say when they're done.
3. Observe. Use neutral probes only (see task-writing).
4. If they are stuck for more than about two minutes, or ask to stop, note it as a failure and move on: "Thanks, that's really useful. Let's move to the next one."
5. Ask the post-task ease question and its follow-up.

## 4. Wrap-up (5 min)

- Of everything you did today, what was the hardest part? What was the easiest?
- Is there anything you expected to see that you didn't?
- Any questions for me?

Now answer their questions and explain anything they got stuck on.

## 5. Thanks (1 min)

Thank them, confirm how and when the incentive arrives, and stop recording.

## Observer guidance

- Observers stay muted and off camera.
- One note-taker per session, using the note sheet.
- Debrief for 10 minutes after each session: top three observations, anything to change before the next session (only fix broken prototypes or unclear tasks mid-study, not the design under test).
