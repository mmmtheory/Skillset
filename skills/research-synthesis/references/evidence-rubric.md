# Evidence rubric

Rate each insight. State the count as "n of N participants". Qualitative samples are small, so never convert counts to percentages.

| Rating | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| **Strong** | At least half the participants, and at least three, show it independently. Includes observed behaviour, not only statements. Little or no counter-evidence. |
| **Moderate** | Three or more participants, or fewer than half the sample. Or mostly stated rather than observed. Or some counter-evidence. |
| **Signal** | One or two participants. Worth watching or testing, not acting on alone. List under signals, not insights. |

## Adjust the rating down when

- most support comes from one segment that is over-represented in the sample
- the moderator's question led to it ("Would it help if...?")
- the source is a summary note rather than a transcript or recording
- participants were recruited because they had the problem

## Adjust the rating up when

- the same pattern shows up in a different kind of source (support tickets, analytics, observation)
- participants show the cost of the problem in the session, not just describe it

## Saying what the research cannot tell you

Every report ends with what this sample cannot answer. Typical limits:

- how common the pattern is in the wider user base
- segments not represented in the sample
- behaviour over time, if every session was a single conversation
- what people would actually do, where the evidence is only what they said they would do
