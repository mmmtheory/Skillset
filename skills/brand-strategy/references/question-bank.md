# Question bank

Pick at most five, only where the answer would change the positioning. Always attach a proposed default.

> **Who do you most want to win?** I'd assume owner-operators of small cafes rather than chains. Right?

## Business and audience

- Who pays, and who uses? Are they the same person?
- Which customers do you most want more of, and which would you happily lose?
- What do customers do today if they don't use you, including doing nothing?
- Why did your best customers choose you over the alternative? (Their words if possible.)
- What do you do that a competitor could not copy within a year?

## Proof

- What can you show today that backs up the difference: a number, a process, a guarantee, a person?
- What do reviews, support tickets or sales calls say again and again?

## Ambition and limits

- Where should the brand be in three years: same audience deeper, or a wider audience?
- Is there a parent brand, partner or regulator the brand must sit alongside?
- What must not change: the name, a colour people know, a promise already made?

## Rebrand

- What do people already associate with the brand, good and bad?
- What triggered the rebrand: a new audience, a merger, a reputation problem, or boredom inside the company?
- Which existing assets carry recognition you would be sorry to lose?

## Voice

- Show me a message you were proud of and one you regret. What was the difference?
- Where does the brand speak most often: product, support, social, sales?

## Questions not to ask

- "What are your brand values?" (Ask for choices and behaviour instead.)
- Anything already answered in the files.
- Preferences you can set as a default and let them correct.
- Open prompts with no default ("Tell me about your brand").
