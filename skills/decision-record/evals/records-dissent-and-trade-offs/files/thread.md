#design-nav thread, 14 to 21 October

Mere (Product Designer): Proposal for the app nav. Swap the hamburger menu for a bottom tab bar with four tabs: Home, Pay, Rewards, Account. Rewards is our second most used area and it's two taps deep right now.

Tom (Android lead): I'd rather keep a drawer on Android. It's what Material does and our Android users are used to it.

Sina (Support lead): We get a steady trickle of "where are my points" tickets. Anything that surfaces Rewards helps.

Mere: Prototype test last week: 4 of 5 people found their points with the tabs. With the current menu, 1 of 5 did.

Jo (iOS lead): Tabs are easy on iOS. Settings would have to move under Account.

Tom: Fine, as long as we look at Android retention after release.

Rawiri (Head of Product): Thanks all. We're going with the four-tab bar on both platforms, shipping in the November release. Settings moves into Account. Tom, noted on Android, let's revisit if Android 30-day retention drops after release.

Mere: I'll update the frames by 28 Oct.
