# DR-0003: Keep the hamburger menu for v3

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2025-03-02
**Decider:** Head of Product

## Decision
The v3 app keeps the hamburger menu.

## Context
v3 had a fixed scope and the nav change was out of it.

## Options
### A. Keep the menu (chosen)
### B. Bottom tabs

## Evidence
- **Judgement:** Not enough time to test tabs before v3. (Head of Product)

## Trade-offs
Rewards stays two taps deep.

## Dissent
None recorded.

## Consequences
- None

## Revisit when
- After the v3 release, when nav can be tested
