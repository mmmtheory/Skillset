# Fingerprints of generated copy

Each of these on its own is fine somewhere. Together, or in interface copy, they make text read as machine-written. Phrases are quoted as code so they are easy to search for.

## Punctuation and rhythm

- **Em and en dashes** used to join clauses. Use two sentences or a comma.
- **Exclamation marks** on outcomes, especially errors.
- **Triplets** of adjectives or benefits in every sentence ("fast, simple and secure").
- **Colon reveals**: a short setup, a colon, then the "answer".
- **Uniform sentence length.** Real writing varies; a two-word sentence is allowed.

## Framing

- `It's not just X, it's Y`, `more than just`, `not X, but Y` used to sound profound.
- Rhetorical questions as openers: `Ever wondered why...?`
- Announcing instead of saying: `We're excited to announce`, `Introducing`.

## Stock phrases

`delve`, `tapestry`, `testament to`, `it's worth noting`, `in today's fast-paced world`, `game-changer`, `elevate`, `unlock the power of`, `seamless`, `seamlessly`, `effortlessly`, `embark on`, `navigate the complexities`, `harness`, `supercharge`, `look no further`, `rest assured`, `cutting-edge`, `best-in-class`, `world-class`, `empower`, `at your fingertips`, `take it to the next level`, `the future of`, `leverage`, `synergy`, `holistic`, `robust`, `furthermore`, `moreover`.

## Interface tells

- `Oops!`, `Uh oh`, `Something went wrong`, `An error occurred` with nothing after it.
- Buttons labelled `Submit`, `Continue`, `OK`, `Click here`, `Learn more` without context.
- Empty states that say `Nothing here yet!` and stop.
- Success messages that celebrate instead of confirm: `Woohoo! You're all set!`
- Emoji standing in for meaning.

## Replacements

| Generated | Human |
| --- | --- |
| `Oops! Something went wrong.` | "Couldn't save your changes. Check your connection and try again." |
| `Unlock the power of seamless invoicing.` | "Send an invoice in under a minute." |
| `It's not just an app, it's a movement.` | (Cut it. Say what the app does.) |
| `Submit` | "Send application" |
| `Nothing here yet!` | "No invoices yet. Create your first one to get paid." |
| `Woohoo! You're all set!` | "Your account is ready." |
| `We're thrilled to introduce dark mode.` | "Dark mode is here. Turn it on in Settings." |
