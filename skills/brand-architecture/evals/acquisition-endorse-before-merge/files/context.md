# Acquisition: Sprout

- We are Larkin, a mid-sized HR software company. Our brand is well known with HR managers at companies of 200+ staff.
- We just bought Sprout, a payroll app loved by small businesses (under 20 staff). Sprout has 30,000 customers and a strong reputation for friendliness.
- Leadership wants to rename Sprout to "Larkin Payroll" in the next quarter.
